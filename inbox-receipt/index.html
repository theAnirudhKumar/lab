Inbox Receipt

Your email as a receipt, including the Reply-All Tax: what pile-on replies cost everyone who had to read them. Read in your browser, one header at a time. Message bodies are skipped.

Drop a Gmail Takeout .mbox, or .eml filesGoogle Takeout, Mail only. Or click to choose.

A pile-on is a reply or forward with 5 or more people on it. The tax is people times reading seconds, an estimate. The rate is applied to everyone, so a blended average is fine.

Your receipt appears here.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no tracking.
