Fitness Wrapped

Your year of movement as a card you can post. Works with a Strava export or an Apple Health export. Read in your browser, never uploaded.

Drop Strava activities.csv or Apple Health export.xmlStrava: Settings, My Account, Download or delete your account. Apple Health: Health app, your profile, Export All Health Data. Or click to choose.

Apple Health files can be very large. The browser reads only the workout lines and skips the rest. Distances come from the export as recorded.

Your card appears here.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no tracking.
