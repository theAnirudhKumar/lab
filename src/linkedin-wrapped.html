LinkedIn Wrapped

Your LinkedIn year as a card: new connections, posts, reactions and comments. Built from your own data export, in your browser. The card shows totals only, never anyone's name.

Drop Connections.csv (plus Shares, Reactions, Comments if you have them)LinkedIn: Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data. Pick Connections and your posts, then select the CSV files. Or click to choose.

Files are recognised by their column names, so rename nothing. Only counts and the most common company and role words are used.
