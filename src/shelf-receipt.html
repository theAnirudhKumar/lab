Shelf Receipt

Your reading or your films for the year, as a receipt. Works with a Goodreads library export or a Letterboxd diary export. Read in your browser, never uploaded.

Goodreads: counts books on the Read shelf with a Date Read. Letterboxd: uses the diary, by the date you watched.
