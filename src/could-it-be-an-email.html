Could It Have Been an Email?

One score for your calendar: the share of your meeting time that probably could have been an email. Compare it with your friends. Nothing leaves your browser.

A meeting is flagged when it has 6 or more people, runs 30 minutes or more and has no written agenda in the invite. Recurring meetings of 8 or more people with no agenda are flagged too. It is a rule of thumb, so treat the score as a conversation starter.
